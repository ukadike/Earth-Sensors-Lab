# Mission

Earth Sensors Lab is an open research laboratory for accessible STEAM education.

We investigate how environmental sensing, creative technology, accessibility, AI,
community science, and multisensory learning can expand who gets to participate
in STEAM — not just who gets to watch it happen.

Earth Sensors Lab is a public-interest research lab studying the
relationship between environmental sensing, accessibility, and STEAM
education. It asks how students, teachers, disabled learners, and
communities can participate in scientific observation when sensors, data,
AI, gardens, sound, touch, and local knowledge are treated as parts of one
learning system.

This is not just about teaching STEM. It is about redesigning who gets to
observe, measure, interpret, and explain the world. See
[RESEARCH_AGENDA.md](RESEARCH_AGENDA.md) for the full research thesis and
the seven pillars this mission is organized around.

## Why a laboratory, not a program

Earth Sensors Lab is not a school program, an after-school club, or a paid
enrichment vendor. It is a research initiative under [Small Systems
Lab](https://ukadike.github.io/Small-Systems-Lab/) that:

- builds low-cost environmental sensing prototypes,
- documents methods so they can be reused and critiqued,
- publishes open research questions instead of closed answers,
- develops curriculum as a research artifact, not a finished product,
- evaluates its own accessibility claims rather than assuming them, and
- prepares pilots with schools, universities, museums, libraries, and
  community partners as research and learning sites.

## What "research" means here

A research initiative asks questions it does not yet know the answer to, and
says so in public. Earth Sensors Lab commits to:

- **No fabrication.** We do not claim completed studies, finished pilots, or
  results we do not have. Unfinished work is labeled `proposed`, `in
  development`, `prototype`, or `OPEN QUESTION` — not described as done.
- **Reusable documentation.** Every module, prototype, and schema is written
  so another lab, teacher, or contributor could pick it up.
- **Accessibility as a research question, not a feature.** We ask *how* sensing
  and data can be made available through touch, sound, sight, and language —
  not whether to bother.
- **Funded through public-interest channels.** Grants, fellowships, university
  partnerships, foundations, and sponsors — not tuition or per-student fees.
  See [PARTNERSHIP_MODEL.md](PARTNERSHIP_MODEL.md).

## Who this is for

Students and educators, yes — but also university researchers, accessibility
specialists, museum and library educators, community science organizers, and
open-source contributors who want to build on the same tools. See
[README.md](README.md) for the full picture of who this serves and what it
builds.

## Relationship to Small Systems Lab

Earth Sensors Lab is one research branch of Small Systems Lab, which studies
public-interest systems built from care, evidence, access, and accountable
rules. Earth Sensors Lab applies that method to environmental sensing and
STEAM education specifically. See [SSL-METHOD.md](SSL-METHOD.md).
