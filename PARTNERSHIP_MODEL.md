# Partnership Model

Earth Sensors Lab is not primarily a paid enrichment vendor.

We are building a nonprofit / public-interest research model. Schools,
universities, museums, libraries, and community sites may participate as
pilot partners, research collaborators, field sites, curriculum partners, or
implementation hosts.

Funding may come from grants, fellowships, universities, foundations,
sponsors, public agencies, or philanthropic partners — not from tuition or
per-student fees charged to families or schools.

This distinction exists because of a real implementation lesson. See
[/pilots/pentaville-case-note.md](pilots/pentaville-case-note.md) for what
that conversation taught us about how this model needs to be explained.

## Partner types

| Partner type | What they contribute | What they get |
|---|---|---|
| **School pilot partner** | Classroom access, teacher time, student participation (with consent) | Curriculum, prototype sensor kits, teacher support, co-authorship on findings |
| **University research partner** | Research design support, evaluation capacity, institutional review, student researchers | Field sites, co-published findings, prototype testbeds |
| **Museum / library partner** | Public-facing space, visitor access, exhibit context | Exhibit-ready modules, community observatory templates |
| **Community garden partner** | Physical site, local ecological knowledge, ongoing stewardship | Sensor kits, observatory support, data ownership over their own site |
| **Accessibility advisor** | Expertise in blind/low-vision, tactile, and assistive-technology design | Direct influence over prototype and curriculum design, credited authorship |
| **Curriculum partner** | Subject-matter review, classroom testing, adaptation notes | Co-developed modules credited to their contribution |
| **Hardware / software collaborator** | Engineering time, code, documentation, testing | Open documentation credit, shared prototype ownership |
| **Funder / sponsor** | Grants, fellowships, in-kind support | Transparent reporting, credited support, no editorial control over research findings |

## What a partnership is not

- Not a tuition-based enrichment program.
- Not a vendor relationship where a school purchases a fixed deliverable.
- Not a data-extraction arrangement — see
  [ETHICS_AND_CONSENT.md](ETHICS_AND_CONSENT.md) for data ownership and
  consent rules.
- Not a claim of an existing, funded partnership unless one is actually in
  place. Any partner named in this repository without a signed agreement is
  marked `proposed` or `AWAITING FRAGMENT`.

## How a partnership starts

1. Initial conversation about research fit — which research question(s) the
   site is positioned to help investigate.
2. Review of [PILOT_MODEL.md](PILOT_MODEL.md) and the relevant template in
   [/pilots/](pilots/) for that partner type.
3. Consent and ethics review per [ETHICS_AND_CONSENT.md](ETHICS_AND_CONSENT.md).
4. A written partnership note (see templates in [/pilots/](pilots/)) — plain
   language, no fee schedule framed as tuition.
5. Funding identified or applied for before the pilot begins, wherever
   possible — schools should not be asked to carry the financial burden of
   experimentation.
