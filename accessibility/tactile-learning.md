# Tactile Learning

Design notes for tactile diagrams, tactile data cards, and hands-on
non-visual learning materials across Earth Sensors Lab.

## Research question

*Can sound and touch make environmental data more understandable?* (See
[RESEARCH_AGENDA.md](../RESEARCH_AGENDA.md).)

## Where tactile design applies

- **Tactile Data Card System** (see [PROTOTYPES.md](../PROTOTYPES.md)) —
  printable, embossable/braille-compatible cards representing sensor data
  trends, concept-stage.
- **Curriculum activities** involving physical sites (garden beds, soil,
  plants) — designed to be explored by touch as a primary, not secondary,
  mode. See [module-01-observation.md](../curriculum/module-01-observation.md)
  and [module-03-soil-and-water.md](../curriculum/module-03-soil-and-water.md).
- **Physical sensor kit labeling** — distinct tactile shapes or textures per
  sensor type, an open hardware-design item (see
  [/hardware/sensor-kit-overview.md](../hardware/sensor-kit-overview.md)).

## Design intent for tactile data cards

- Represent a single data trend (e.g., a week of soil-moisture readings) as
  a raised-line or embossable graph, printable on standard tactile-graphics
  equipment where available.
- Pair every tactile card with a plain-language, spoken-equivalent summary,
  so the card is not the only way to access that trend's meaning.
- Avoid encoding meaning purely through fine raised-line detail that may be
  hard to distinguish by touch — favor larger, distinct raised regions and
  textures over fine gradients.

## Status

Concept documentation only. No tactile card has been produced, printed, or
tested with a tactile-graphics user. This is recorded as `in development`
concept work, not a validated design.

## Open items

- No accessibility advisor with tactile-graphics expertise has reviewed this
  design intent yet.
- Production method (thermoform, swell paper, 3D print) is undecided —
  `OPEN QUESTION`.
