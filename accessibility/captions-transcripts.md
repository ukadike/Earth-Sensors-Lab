# Captions and Transcripts

Requirements for captioning and transcribing any audio or video material
produced by Earth Sensors Lab.

## Requirements

- Every video must have accurate, synchronized captions before publication.
- Every audio clip (including sonification demos, see
  [sonification.md](sonification.md)) must have an accompanying text
  transcript or plain-language description.
- Captions and transcripts must be reviewed for accuracy, not
  auto-generated and published without review.
- Where a video or audio piece is instructional (e.g., a hardware build
  guide demo), the transcript should be usable as a standalone text
  instruction set — not just a caption of what was said.

## Why this matters here specifically

Earth Sensors Lab documents hardware builds (see [/hardware/](../hardware/))
and may eventually produce demo video/audio for dashboards and
sonification tools. Any of these without captions/transcripts would
directly contradict the accessibility baseline in
[ACCESSIBILITY.md](../ACCESSIBILITY.md) and exclude Deaf and hard-of-hearing
participants from exactly the multisensory design this lab claims to
practice.

## Status

No video or audio content currently exists in this repository. This document
sets the requirement in advance so future contributions meet it from the
start, per the "design multisensory from the start" principle in
[accessibility-principles.md](accessibility-principles.md).
