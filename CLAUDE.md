# Claude Code Instructions for Earth Sensors Lab

This repository is a research and prototype repository for accessible STEAM
education.

Do not reduce the project to a children's workshop, school club, or paid
enrichment program.

Always preserve the research framing:

- accessible STEAM education
- environmental sensing
- multisensory learning
- community science
- public-interest technology
- inclusive curriculum
- low-cost instrumentation
- grant-ready documentation

## When editing, prioritize

1. clarity
2. accessibility
3. research credibility
4. open-source usefulness
5. partnership readiness
6. grant readiness
7. future software/hardware implementation

Use plain language, but do not flatten the intellectual ambition.

## Every new component should include

- purpose
- audience
- research question
- accessibility notes
- implementation notes
- schema card if applicable (see [SCHEMA_CARD.md](SCHEMA_CARD.md))

## No fabrication

Do not invent fake results, fake pilots, fake partners, or completed studies.

Use "pilot," "prototype," "research question," "proposed," and "in
development" when appropriate. Where an artifact is not physically present
in the repository, create an index entry only, tagged `AWAITING FRAGMENT` —
do not describe, summarize, or infer its contents.

## Tone rules

Tone should be clear, serious, warm, research-oriented, institution-ready,
accessible, not overhyped, not corporate, not childish.

Avoid: "fun STEM camp," "after-school business," "edtech product," "AI
replaces teachers," "data surveillance," "charity for disabled students."

Prefer: "accessible STEAM research," "public-interest lab," "community
observatory," "multisensory learning," "prototype," "pilot," "field
research," "teacher-supported inquiry."

## Locked visual system

`variables.css` is a shared visual-language token system used across Small
Systems Lab, Echo, Omoluabi, and Umada. Do not redesign it from this repo;
treat it as locked. `site.css` may extend layout on top of it.
