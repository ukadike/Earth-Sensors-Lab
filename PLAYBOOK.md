# Earth Sensors Lab — Playbook

## Playbook Summary

The Earth Sensors Lab Playbook contains the instructions required to establish and operate an Earth Sensors Lab cohort.

It is organized around five connected activities:

### Build the Instruments
Students learn the relevant science and construct observation tools.

### Build the Observatory
Individual instruments become parts of one shared sensing environment.

### Power the System
Students generate, store, measure, and allocate renewable energy.

### Interrogate the Evidence
Students compare observations, question measurements, investigate uncertainty, and use Omoluabi to navigate codified relationships within the system.

### Open the Lab
Students transform their work into an accessible public installation and teach others what they have learned.
