# Grant Language

Reusable language for funders, grant applications, and institutional
partnership conversations describing Earth Sensors Lab.

## Core statement

> Earth Sensors Lab advances accessible STEAM education by developing
> research-based tools and methods for environmental sensing, multisensory
> data interpretation, and community-based scientific observation.
>
> The project combines creative technology, accessibility, environmental
> literacy, and public-interest research to support learners who are often
> excluded from conventional STEM pathways.

## Supporting language

> Earth Sensors Lab is a research initiative under Small Systems Lab. It
> studies how accessible sensing systems can support STEAM learning across
> classrooms, gardens, museums, libraries, and community sites — asking, in
> particular, how blind and low-vision learners can participate fully in
> environmental data observation, how sound and touch can make data more
> understandable, and how AI can support student reflection without
> replacing teacher judgment.

> Earth Sensors Lab partners with schools and institutions as research and
> learning sites. It is not a fee-for-service school vendor. Its goal is to
> develop, test, document, and share accessible STEAM methods through funded
> research, grants, fellowships, and public-interest partnerships.

## Framing notes for grant writers

- Always name the research questions (see
  [RESEARCH_AGENDA.md](../RESEARCH_AGENDA.md)) specifically — avoid vague
  "STEM enrichment" language that a reviewer might read as an
  already-existing enrichment program rather than a research initiative.
- State plainly that hardware and software are prototypes, and curriculum is
  in development — see [PROTOTYPES.md](../PROTOTYPES.md) and
  [ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md) — rather than implying a finished toolkit.
- Cite the accessibility baseline in [ACCESSIBILITY.md](../ACCESSIBILITY.md)
  as a core research commitment, not a compliance afterthought.
- Do not cite specific pilot outcomes, partner names, or funding amounts
  that are not already documented elsewhere in this repository as real.
