# Glossary

Terms used across Earth Sensors Lab documentation.

**Accessible STEAM research** — this lab's framing of its own work: STEAM
education and research designed for accessibility from the start, not
retrofitted. See [ACCESSIBILITY.md](../ACCESSIBILITY.md).

**Community observatory** — an ongoing environmental observation site
(garden, schoolyard, public site) stewarded by its community, using Earth
Sensors Lab curriculum and/or prototype kits. See
[module-06-community-observatory.md](../curriculum/module-06-community-observatory.md).

**Findings** — recorded evidence from a completed pilot or study, tagged
`EMERGING` or `PROBABLE`. See
[/research/findings-template.md](../research/findings-template.md).

**In development** — status label meaning agreed and being prepared, but not
yet running or complete.

**Multisensory learning** — learning designed around touch, sound, sight,
and language as co-equal channels, not sight-first with accommodations
added later. See [accessibility-principles.md](../accessibility/accessibility-principles.md).

**Open Question / OPEN QUESTION** — a genuinely unresolved question,
explicitly not answered by anything else in the repository.

**Pilot** — a bounded research collaboration between Earth Sensors Lab and a
real site partner, testing prototypes or curriculum. See
[PILOT_MODEL.md](../PILOT_MODEL.md).

**Prototype** — an early-stage hardware or software concept, documented but
not necessarily built, tested, or field-validated. See
[PROTOTYPES.md](../PROTOTYPES.md).

**Proposed** — status label meaning discussed but not yet agreed, funded, or
scheduled.

**Public-interest lab** — this lab's framing of its organizational identity:
funded through grants and partnerships for public benefit, not tuition or
per-student fees. See [PARTNERSHIP_MODEL.md](../PARTNERSHIP_MODEL.md).

**Research question** — one of the guiding open questions in
[RESEARCH_AGENDA.md](../RESEARCH_AGENDA.md) that this lab's work is
organized around answering, honestly and incrementally.

**Rule-based intelligence** — the Small Systems Lab method's approach to
AI/automation: exposing rules and flags rather than opaque conclusions. See
[SSL-METHOD.md](../SSL-METHOD.md) and
[/software/ai-reflection-assistant.md](../software/ai-reflection-assistant.md).

**Schema card** — the required structural summary (purpose, audience,
research question, accessibility requirements, inputs, outputs, materials,
data collected, consent needs, risks, next steps) for every major component.
See [SCHEMA_CARD.md](../SCHEMA_CARD.md).

**Sonification** — representing data as sound so trends are audible. See
[sonification.md](../accessibility/sonification.md).

**Tactile data card** — a printable/embossable physical representation of a
data trend, for non-visual reading. See
[tactile-learning.md](../accessibility/tactile-learning.md).
