# Case Note: Pentaville

## What happened

An early conversation with Pentaville Schools (Nigeria) raised a natural and
reasonable question: was Earth Sensors Lab a paid enrichment program?

The Pentaville conversation revealed an important implementation challenge:
schools may understandably ask whether a new STEAM initiative is a paid
program, while Earth Sensors Lab is positioned as a research and
public-interest initiative seeking external support. That is a completely
fair question for any school to ask when approached by a new program — most
STEAM enrichment offerings *are* fee-for-service, so assuming the same about
Earth Sensors Lab was a reasonable default, not a misunderstanding on the
school's part.

## What this case note is for

This case note helps clarify the need for better partnership language,
funding explanations, and research onboarding materials. It exists to
improve how Earth Sensors Lab explains itself — not to characterize
Pentaville or any school's response as a problem to be corrected.

## What changed as a result

- [PARTNERSHIP_MODEL.md](../PARTNERSHIP_MODEL.md) now states plainly, up
  front, that Earth Sensors Lab is not primarily a fee-for-service vendor,
  and names the actual funding channels (grants, fellowships, universities,
  foundations, sponsors, public agencies, philanthropic partners).
- [/docs/grant-language.md](../docs/grant-language.md) and
  [/docs/one-page-summary.md](../docs/one-page-summary.md) were drafted so
  that future school conversations can point to concrete, funder-facing
  language rather than relying on an in-person explanation each time.
- [PILOT_MODEL.md](../PILOT_MODEL.md) now describes funding identification
  as an explicit early phase of any pilot, before a site is asked to commit
  time or resources.

## Status

No Pentaville pilot is currently active or described elsewhere in this
repository as completed, ongoing, or planned — this case note documents the
implementation lesson only. Any future partnership with Pentaville or
similar schools would be a new, separate conversation using the updated
partnership materials above.
