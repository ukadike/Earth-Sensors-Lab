# ITP/IMA + high-school six-week pilot discussion

**Status:** Proposed discussion draft. No ITP/IMA or high-school partnership is confirmed.

This Earth Sensors Lab concept outlines a possible six-week accessible STEAM pilot for ITP/IMA and a high-school partner. It begins with a local outdoor observation question, combines sensor or manual measurements with field notes, and asks students to represent findings in a form that includes text alternatives and a portable Schema Card.

- [Download the PowerPoint discussion deck](itp-ima-high-school-six-week-pilot-discussion.pptx)
- [Earth Sensors Lab](https://ukadike.github.io/earth-sensors-lab/)
- [Accessible by Design](https://ukadike.github.io/accessible-by-design-prototyping/)

The six-week sequence is a draft for discussion. The school partner, site, cohort, schedule, staffing, sensor setup, and resources remain open.

The example question in the deck is: “Where does garden soil dry first, and what can our readings actually tell us?” It is one possible inquiry, not a fixed curriculum requirement.
