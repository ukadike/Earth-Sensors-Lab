# Community Garden Pilot Template

A template for structuring a community garden pilot partnership. Copy this
into a new, site-specific file when a real pilot is being planned — do not
fill in hypothetical details here.

```markdown
# Community Garden Pilot: [Site Name]

Status: proposed | in development | pilot | closed

## Fit and framing
- Research question(s) this pilot investigates: [link to research-questions.md]
- Site stewards / community contacts: [named once confirmed]
- Funding source: [grant / fellowship / sponsor / in-kind]

## Consent and ethics
- Site steward agreement, including data ownership terms: [date]
- Community consent/notice process: [description]
- Accessibility accommodations identified: [list]

## Design
- Prototype(s) / curriculum module(s) used: [links, likely including
  module-06-community-observatory.md]
- Observatory scope (what's measured, how often, by whom): [description]
- Duration: [dates]

## Implementation notes
- [Ongoing log of what actually happened, dated entries]

## Evaluation
- See /research/pilot-evaluation.md framework
- Findings recorded at: [link, once written]

## Continuation or close
- Outcome: [continuing / expanded / closed]
- Who continues stewarding the site after this pilot: [description]
```

## Notes specific to community garden contexts

- Data ownership defaults to the site/community, per the community-ownership
  principle in [ETHICS_AND_CONSENT.md](../ETHICS_AND_CONSENT.md) — what's
  shared publicly versus kept local should be a community decision, not
  imposed by Earth Sensors Lab.
- This template is the natural pairing for
  [module-06-community-observatory.md](../curriculum/module-06-community-observatory.md)
  and the proposed Community Observatory Kit / Offline Garden Lab in
  [PROTOTYPES.md](../PROTOTYPES.md).
- Local ecological knowledge held by site stewards should be recorded and
  credited alongside sensor data, not treated as secondary to it.
