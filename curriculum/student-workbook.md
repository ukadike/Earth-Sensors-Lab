# Student Workbook

A printable, low-ink, plain-language companion to the curriculum modules in
[/curriculum/](.). Each section below corresponds to a module and can be
printed or read independently.

## How to use this workbook

- Each page is designed to work on paper, on screen with a screen reader, or
  spoken aloud by a partner or teacher.
- No section requires color to understand — see
  [ACCESSIBILITY.md](../ACCESSIBILITY.md).
- Fill in only what applies to your activity; skipped sections are fine.
