3I/ATLAS — Trajectory Case Study

This p5.js diagram is an educational, not precision-navigation visualization. It represents the object's hyperbolic passage through the Solar System and key NASA-reported milestones. A later data build will replace the pedagogical path with versioned JPL Horizons ephemeris snapshots.

Scientific boundary: 3I/ATLAS is a comet on a hyperbolic trajectory. NASA reports perihelion on October 30, 2025 at about 1.4 AU from the Sun. The visualization does not imply that local Earth-sensor anomalies were caused by the comet.
