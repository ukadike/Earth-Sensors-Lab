Full, versioned list of Earth Sensors Lab's guiding research questions, with
status tags. Status moves from `OPEN QUESTION` → `EMERGING` (early prototype
or single-pilot evidence) → `PROBABLE` (supported by more than one pilot or
study) as real evidence accumulates — never by assertion.

## Notes on status

No question above has moved past `OPEN QUESTION` as of this writing — no
pilot has yet been run to generate evidence. This table will be updated only
when a specific pilot or study, recorded via
[findings-template.md](findings-template.md), produces evidence bearing on a
question.

## Adding a question

New research questions should:

1. Be genuinely open — not a restatement of something already answered
   elsewhere in the repository.
2. Name at least one prototype, curriculum module, or pilot type that could
   generate evidence toward it.
3. Be added here and cross-referenced from [RESEARCH_AGENDA.md](../RESEARCH_AGENDA.md).
