# Knowledge Contributions

Earth Sensors Lab aims to contribute knowledge in five areas:

1. **Accessible STEAM pedagogy** — how observation-first, multisensory
   teaching sequences work in real classrooms and community sites.
2. **Multisensory environmental data design** — reusable patterns for
   representing the same environmental data as sound, touch, image, and
   text.
3. **Low-cost sensing for schools and communities** — validated (once
   tested), affordable hardware configurations that don't assume a lab
   budget.
4. **Inclusive research partnership models** — how to run school/community
   research pilots that don't reproduce the "vendor" framing this lab
   explicitly rejects (see [PARTNERSHIP_MODEL.md](../PARTNERSHIP_MODEL.md)).
5. **Public-interest AI for scientific reflection** — a working example of
   AI used to support, not replace, student and teacher reasoning (see
   [/software/ai-boundaries.md](../software/ai-boundaries.md)).

## Status of each contribution area

None of the five areas above has produced a validated contribution yet — no
pilot has concluded, no prototype has been field-tested, and no partnership
model has been run through a full cycle. This page states *intended*
contribution areas so future work can be organized around them and measured
against them, not so past work can be claimed against them prematurely.

## How a contribution gets made

A knowledge contribution becomes real when:

1. A [pilot](../PILOT_MODEL.md) or study actually runs.
2. Its outcome is recorded honestly via
   [findings-template.md](findings-template.md), including where it failed
   or was inconclusive.
3. The finding is generalized only as far as the evidence supports — see the
   `OPEN QUESTION` → `EMERGING` → `PROBABLE` status vocabulary in
   [research-questions.md](research-questions.md).
4. The finding is written back into the relevant contribution area above,
   with a link to its source pilot or study — never asserted here without
   that link.

## Why five areas, not one

Treating these as five distinct contribution areas (rather than one general
"we help kids learn science" claim) keeps each area separately falsifiable:
a pilot might succeed at accessible pedagogy while revealing that a specific
hardware configuration doesn't hold up outdoors, and that is exactly the
kind of differentiated finding this structure is meant to preserve rather
than average away.
