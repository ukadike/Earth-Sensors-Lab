# Literature Map

This page maps the fields Earth Sensors Lab's research agenda draws on. It is
a map of *fields and prior work to consult*, not a completed literature
review — no specific citations are asserted here, since none have been
verified against this repository's actual research process yet. Populating
this with specific sources is `AWAITING FRAGMENT`.

## Relevant fields

- **Environmental science education** — school- and community-based
  environmental monitoring, citizen/community science pedagogy.
- **Accessible STEM/STEAM education** — inclusive design for blind and
  low-vision learners in data-heavy science instruction.
- **Data sonification** — auditory display research, as applied to
  environmental and scientific data specifically.
- **Tactile graphics and tactile learning** — tactile diagram design, tactile
  data representation for STEM.
- **Human-AI interaction in education** — AI-supported reflection and
  pattern-finding tools that preserve teacher and learner agency.
- **Community-based participatory research** — methods for research with
  (not on) schools and community sites, informing [ETHICS_AND_CONSENT.md](../ETHICS_AND_CONSENT.md).
- **Low-cost scientific instrumentation** — open hardware and DIY sensor
  design for education contexts.
- **Climate and environmental literacy curricula** — existing frameworks for
  teaching climate/environmental concepts across age groups.
- **Cross-cultural and place-based science education** — integrating local
  ecological knowledge into formal STEAM curricula.

## How this map is used

- [RESEARCH_AGENDA.md](../RESEARCH_AGENDA.md) questions should each be
  traceable to at least one field above.
- Curriculum modules cite relevant fields in their "research notes" section
  rather than inventing pedagogical claims.
- As specific sources are actually reviewed and verified, they will be added
  here with full citations — not before.
