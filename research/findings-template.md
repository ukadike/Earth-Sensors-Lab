# Findings Template

Use this template to record evidence from a completed pilot or study
activity. Copy this structure into a new, dated file when real findings
exist — do not fill in this template with hypothetical or illustrative
findings.

```markdown
# Finding: [short title]

Date: [YYYY-MM-DD]
Pilot / study: [link to the specific pilot note in /pilots/ or study protocol]
Research question(s) addressed: [link to research-questions.md entries]
Status of this finding: EMERGING | PROBABLE

## What was tried

[Prototype(s)/module(s), site, duration, number of participants.]

## What was observed

[Plain description. Separate direct observation from interpretation.]

## Limits of this evidence

[Sample size, duration, confounds, anything that limits generalization.]

## Accessibility outcomes

[What worked, what didn't, for the specific accommodations used.]

## Partner feedback

[What the site partner said about the findings before publication.]

## Suggested status change

[Does this move a research-questions.md entry from OPEN QUESTION to
EMERGING, or from EMERGING to PROBABLE? State the reasoning; do not just
assert the new status.]

## What would change next time

[Concrete adjustments for a future pilot.]
```

## Status

No findings have been recorded yet — this repository documents prototypes
and methods that have not yet been piloted with real participants. This file
exists so that when a pilot does run, its findings have a consistent,
honest place to live.
