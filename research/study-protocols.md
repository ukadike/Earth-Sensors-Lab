# Study Protocols

This document describes how a formal study or pilot evaluation *would* be
run under Earth Sensors Lab, if and when one is funded, staffed, and
consented to. No study protocol below has been executed. This is a template
and set of principles, not a study log.

## Principles

- No study begins without the consent process in
  [ETHICS_AND_CONSENT.md](../ETHICS_AND_CONSENT.md) fully in place.
- No study is framed to participants as anything other than what it is —
  research participation, not a service being delivered to them.
- Every study protocol names the specific [research question(s)](research-questions.md)
  it addresses before data collection begins.
- Findings are evidence, not conclusions, until independently corroborated —
  see the status vocabulary in [PILOT_MODEL.md](../PILOT_MODEL.md).

## Protocol template

A study protocol, when written for an actual pilot, should specify:

1. **Research question(s)** — from [research-questions.md](research-questions.md).
2. **Site and partner type** — per [PARTNERSHIP_MODEL.md](../PARTNERSHIP_MODEL.md).
3. **Participants** — who, how many, how recruited, what consent applies.
4. **Method** — what is observed, measured, or collected, and how (e.g., a
   specific curriculum module run with pre/post reflection, or sensor data
   logged over a fixed period).
5. **Data handling** — minimization, storage, anonymization, per
   [ETHICS_AND_CONSENT.md](../ETHICS_AND_CONSENT.md).
6. **Accessibility accommodations** — specific to the participants, per
   [ACCESSIBILITY.md](../ACCESSIBILITY.md).
7. **Evaluation plan** — how findings will be assessed, per
   [pilot-evaluation.md](pilot-evaluation.md).
8. **Duration and close-out** — including how/when data is deleted or
   archived, and how findings are returned to the site partner.

## Institutional review

Where a university research partner is involved, that partner's institutional
review board (IRB) process governs in addition to, not instead of, the
principles above. No claim of IRB approval appears anywhere in this
repository unless a specific, real approval exists.
